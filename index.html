Our story

In 2020 we built Kenya's first WhatsApp tax assistant.

UshuruBot helped Kenyans file with the Kenya Revenue Authority from inside WhatsApp. The Authority launched its own chatbot years later.

That pattern is how Afribot works: see what Africa's institutions and markets will need next, and build it first.

  1. 2020UshuruBot launches on WhatsApp.
  2. 2022 to 2024The studio grows into a portfolio of platforms across several sectors.
  3. NowRaising a USD 2–5 million seed round.
Approach

Six stages from idea to launch.

Every venture follows the same pipeline, so each one is technically sound, commercially viable and ready for investors before it ships.

  1. 1

    Define

    Frame the problem, size the market and map the regulation.

  2. 2

    Plan

    Design the architecture, business model and revenue.

  3. 3

    Build

    Develop the product, its integrations and payments.

  4. 4

    Verify

    Test quality, security and performance.

  5. 5

    Review

    Prepare investor and compliance documents and walk through the product.

  6. 6

    Ship

    Launch to production with monitoring and a rollout plan.
