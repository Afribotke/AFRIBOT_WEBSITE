Who We Are

African Ideas.
Global Impact.

Afribot Ventures Ltd is not a software agency. We are not a consultancy. We are Africa's first AI venture studio — a factory of platform companies, each designed to own a vertical, achieve regulatory compliance, and scale across the continent.

Every platform begins with a business model, not a feature list. From M-Pesa-native fintech rails powering Kenya's SACCOs and smallholder farmers, to AI-driven employment platforms and creator economy tools — every venture is grounded in deep local insight, regulatory awareness, and a relentless commitment to inclusion.

Our edge is systems thinking at speed: the ability to design, build, and validate complete platform ecosystems — including compliance documentation, monetisation architecture, and investor materials — faster than any team on the continent.

"Every challenge Africa faces is a platform waiting to be built."
— Geoffrey Koech, Founder & CEO
2020
The UshuruBot Origin

Before KRA launched its own chatbot, Geoffrey Koech built UshuruBot — a WhatsApp-based tax filing assistant for the Kenya Revenue Authority. Built in 2020, it predated KRA's equivalent product by six years. That moment defined Afribot Ventures: a company that sees what Africa needs before the institutions do.

The Visionary

Ideas Generator.
System Architect.

Geoffrey Koech is not a software engineer in the traditional sense — he is something rarer: a systems thinker and ideas architect who conceptualises entire platform ecosystems from first principles. Business models, regulatory compliance, monetisation frameworks, and technical integrations all flow from a single mind before a single line of code is written.

⚡ The UshuruBot Moment — 2020

Geoffrey built UshuruBot, a WhatsApp chatbot that automated KRA tax filing, years before the Kenya Revenue Authority launched its own equivalent product. This wasn't luck — it was pattern recognition at its finest: seeing where Africa's institutions would inevitably arrive, and building there first.

Today Geoffrey leads Afribot Ventures Ltd across a portfolio of 8 platform ventures spanning fintech, agritech, transport, creator economy, employment, and AI infrastructure. Each platform reflects the same founding insight: Africa's most pressing challenges are also its greatest market opportunities.

His approach is rigorous and structured — every build follows a six-stage product pipeline (DEFINE → PLAN → BUILD → VERIFY → REVIEW → SHIP), ensuring ventures are investor-ready, regulator-aware, and user-centred before they reach the market.

Geoffrey identifies publicly as "Africa's AI Epitome" — a bold declaration that reflects not ego, but a genuine belief that the continent's builders must claim their rightful place at the frontier of global technology innovation.

Our Purpose

Why We Build

We believe Africa's next billion people deserve world-class digital infrastructure — built by Africans, for Africans, with the ambition to serve the world.

🔬 Deep Innovation

We don't copy Silicon Valley playbooks. We identify uniquely African problems and engineer platform-native solutions — from M-Pesa rails to USSD-first design, we build for the actual user.

⚖️ Compliance First

Every venture is built with regulatory awareness from day one — CBK, NTSA, SASRA, NEA. Regulatory compliance isn't a barrier; it's a competitive moat.

🌍 Continental Scale

We build for Kenya but architect for Africa. Our platforms are designed with multi-country expansion from the ground up — Nigeria, South Africa, Ghana, and beyond.
